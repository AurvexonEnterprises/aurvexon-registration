1Our services

Aurvexon Talent provides profile screening, CV guidance, job matching, interview preparation and coordination with employers. Registration, vacancy information and basic screening are free.

നിങ്ങളുടെ പ്രൊഫൈൽ പരിശോധിക്കൽ, CV മാർഗനിർദേശം, അനുയോജ്യമായ ജോലികൾ കണ്ടെത്തൽ, interview preparation എന്നിവയിൽ സഹായിക്കുന്നു. Registration-നും basic screening-നും ഫീസ് ഇല്ല.

2When the fee is payable

The fee becomes payable once the employer confirms your selection by email, WhatsApp, phone call or during the interview. The fee must be paid in full before your joining date.

തിരഞ്ഞെടുപ്പ് സ്ഥിരീകരിച്ച ശേഷം, ജോലിയിൽ പ്രവേശിക്കുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് മുഴുവൻ ഫീസും അടയ്ക്കണം.

3Offer letter and joining

Where the employer’s offer letter or joining formalities are coordinated through Aurvexon Talent, they are completed after the fee is paid. We never hold your personal documents or certificates.

4If you do not join

If you do not join, the fee is not refunded. If this happens within 40 days of the originally agreed joining date, we will seek another suitable job in the same salary slab at no additional fee. A higher salary slab requires payment of the fee difference.

Join ചെയ്തില്ലെങ്കിൽ ഫീസ് തിരികെ ലഭിക്കില്ല. പകരം, 40 ദിവസത്തിനുള്ളിൽ അതേ salary slab-ൽ മറ്റൊരു അനുയോജ്യമായ ജോലി കണ്ടെത്താൻ സഹായിക്കും.

540-day replacement support

If, within 40 days of joining, you leave voluntarily or the employer lays you off for company-side reasons, Aurvexon Talent will seek another suitable job in the same salary slab at no additional fee.

Example: You paid ₹4,000 for a ₹20,000 job. A replacement job pays ₹30,000 (fee ₹7,500). You pay the balance of ₹3,500.
  • Not applicable to termination for performance, misconduct, absconding or false information.
  • Applies once per placement, within 40 days; it is not a cash refund.
  • Subject to suitable vacancies and employer selection.
  • Inform us within 7 days of leaving the job.

പ്രകടനം, മോശം പെരുമാറ്റം, absconding അല്ലെങ്കിൽ തെറ്റായ വിവരങ്ങൾ എന്നിവ കാരണം പിരിച്ചുവിട്ടാൽ replacement support ബാധകമല്ല.

6What we do not promise

Aurvexon Talent does not guarantee a job, interview, selection or salary. The final hiring decision rests with the employer. Opportunities depend on vacancies and employer requirements.

7Privacy and consent

  • We collect your details to help with your job search.
  • Your CV and profile are shared only with relevant employers, with your consent.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • You may withdraw consent or request deletion by emailing hello@aurvexon.in.
